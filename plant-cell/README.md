# Printable plant cell model

Download [Plant_Cell_Print_Package.zip](Plant_Cell_Print_Package.zip?raw=true) and extract it to access:

- `Plant_Cell_150mm.stl` — one-piece, flat-backed plant cell relief
- `Plant_Cell_Preview.png` — labeled preview
- `PRINT_README.txt` — printing instructions

Dimensions: approximately **150 × 130 × 11.4 mm**, in millimeters. Suggested starting settings: PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.16–0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill; flat base on the build plate. Supports should not be needed; inspect the sliced preview before printing.

The simplified model includes a cell wall, cell membrane ridge, cytoplasm/base, central vacuole with tonoplast ridge, nucleus and nucleolus, rough and smooth ER, ribosomes, Golgi apparatus, chloroplasts, and mitochondria. Structures are stylized and not to biological scale. Labels appear in the preview rather than on the print.

The mesh passed closed-manifold and positive-volume checks. It has not been physically test-printed or validated in a slicer.
